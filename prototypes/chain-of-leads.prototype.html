Chain of leads: the Track step

Prototype, throwaway, for the ticket "Tracking game: the chain of leads". Nothing here is game code; numbers are guesses to react to.

The question. How does Track play as a chain of leads? How many leads per target size, what kind each one is, how the bounty-hunter window shows and follows them, and how the last lead hands over a fresh position, all inside a 20-40 minute hunt with under a quarter of it empty travel.

The model being tested:

  • Leads sit on the target's real trail. Lead 1 is where SWI's stale sighting already points, and each lead is one stop further along. Two leads per sector: who saw it (at a station), then where it went (the jump record at the gate).
  • The window shows finished leads with what they told you, the current lead with a "Plot course" button, and only a count of the rest.
  • The last lead hands over a fresh position: swibh writes the faction's sighting record, so SWI's own beacon moves to the target's live sector. For a short hot trail after that, the beacon follows the target through gates.
  • SWI's find step fires once you're in the target's sector (proximity), then you scan the target to identify it and the strike begins. The 3-hour find timer still runs underneath.
  • At standing tier 3+, the contact hands over a tracking fob: the last lead becomes a remote ping from the window, with no trip.
